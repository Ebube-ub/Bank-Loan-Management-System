How is my score worked out?

Everyone starts at 600. Then points are added or taken away:

What happens Points
Each installment you pay on time+10 (up to +150)
Each installment you pay late-15
Each installment that is overdue now-40
Each loan you finish paying off+25 (up to +75)
A loan that defaulted (3 missed installments in a row)-150
Using a lot of your total borrowing limit (only while you have a loan)+10 under a quarter, 0 up to half, -20 up to 80%, -40 above
